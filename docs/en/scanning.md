# Scanning

[← Contents](index.md)

**Scanning** adds cards to the collection from photos and scans:

1. Drop files on the area at the top (or click to choose them): photos of single cards, scans of
   whole pages (up to 9 cards, e.g. a binder page) or PDFs. The app tells a single card from a page
   by itself.
2. The app finds the cards, straightens them and recognizes them by name, collector line and art.
3. Review the results: fix the printing if needed, choose finish, condition and location, then
   accept. **Accept confident (≥ 90%)** accepts the sure ones at once.
4. **Add accepted to the collection.** Nothing reaches the collection before this step.

![Scanning](../screenshots/scan.webp)

If a page scan missed cards, use **Missed some cards? Grid…**: give the number of rows and columns,
optionally over a selected area.

## Tips

- **Flatbed scanner:**
  - scan black-bordered cards with the lid closed (white background);
  - white-bordered cards with the lid open in a dark room (black background);
  - a small gap between cards helps.
- **Photos:** one card per photo, from above, on a contrasting matte background, in diffuse light
  without reflections on the card or sleeve.
- **iPhone:** HEIC photos work on macOS. On Windows, set the iPhone to save JPG (Settings → Camera →
  Formats → Most Compatible).
- **Text recognition:** Apple Vision on macOS; RapidOCR (bundled models, offline) on Windows.
